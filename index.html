Why I built this.
I'm trying to solve some of the problems I see around me in my country, one small piece at a time — this dictionary is one of them. It's dedicated to my brother, Muussaa, who passed away in a car accident. We loved each other deeply, and when he was gone, I realized he never belonged only to me — everyone in our community felt the loss too. That's what pushed me to build something for everyone around me, not just for myself. Every word in here is for him, and for anyone trying to learn, connect, and grow.
Maaliif kana hojjadhe.
Rakkoolee biyya kootti argu keessaa tokko tokko, xinnoo xinnoodhaan furuuf carraaqaan jira — jechoonni kun tokko isaanii keessaati. Kun obboleessa koo, Muussaa, kan balaa konkolaataatiin lubbuun darbeef kenname. Wal jaalluu guddaa qabna ture; innis yeroo na biraa deemetti, akka inni qofa naaf hin taane hubadhe — hawaasa keenya keessatti hundinuu gadda dhagahe. Kanatu na kakaase akka waan hawaasa koo hundaaf ta'u ijaaru, kan ana qofaaf hin taane. Jecha as jiru hunda isaaf, akkasumas nama barachuu, walqunnamuu fi guddachuu barbaadu hundaaf kenname.
